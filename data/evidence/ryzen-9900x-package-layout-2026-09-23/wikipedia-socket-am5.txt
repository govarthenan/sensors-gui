## Body text (verbatim extract, intro and heatsink section; navigation, "History" prose
## beyond what's relevant, and references list stripped)

Socket AM5 (LGA 1718) is a zero insertion force flip-chip land grid array (LGA)
CPU socket designed by AMD that is used for AMD Ryzen microprocessors starting
with the Zen 4 microarchitecture. AM5 was launched in September 2022 and is the
successor to AM4.

The Ryzen 7000 series processors were the first AM5 processors. The 7000 series
added support for PCI Express 5.0 and DDR5.

### Heatsink

The AM5 socket specifies four holes for fastening the heatsink to the
motherboard to be placed at the corners of a rectangle with a lateral length of
54x90 mm, as well as UNC #6-32 screw threads for the backplate, identical to
those of the preceding AM4 socket. Furthermore, the Z-height of the CPU package
is kept the same as that of AM4, for backward compatibility of heatsinks.
