"Lift it up to reveal the card, with coolant tubing running into a layer below. This layer
houses the 240 mm radiator and two Gentle Typhoon 120 mm fans."

"The MSI SUPRIM Liquid X RTX 4090 employs an AIO liquid cooler for the GPU and memory, while
a single fan cools the remainder of the card. Coolant tubing connects the pump block to a
240 mm radiator, which is outfitted with a pair of high-quality Gentle Typhoon 120 mm fans."

"This appears to be a radiator made of aluminium. Rubberized frame mounts absorb vibrations
on the fans."

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:
1. Confirms, independently of KitGuru and WCCFTech, the basic layout: ONE fan on the card
   itself ("a single fan cools the remainder of the card") and TWO fans on the 240 mm
   radiator.
2. Names the radiator fans "Gentle Typhoon" 120 mm fans. THIS DISAGREES with WCCFTech's
   naming of "MSI's latest MEG Silent GALE P12 fans" for the same radiator
   (data/evidence/rtx4090-suprim-layout-2026-09-23/wccftech-suprim-liquidx-teardown.txt) and
   with KitGuru calling them "MSI's included Silent Gale fans" (see
   kitguru-suprim-liquidx-review.txt). "Gentle Typhoon" is normally a Scythe-branded
   enthusiast fan line; no other source corroborates that name for this card. Kept as stated,
   flagged as the lowest-confidence of the three naming claims, not resolved.
3. Does not name the AIO manufacturer in this excerpt; a separate KitGuru statement names
   Cooler Master (see kitguru-suprim-liquidx-review.txt) — this Guru3D page neither confirms
   nor contradicts that.
4. Gives no RPM numbers and no statement on which fan is "Fan 1"/"Fan 2" in any monitoring
   tool.
