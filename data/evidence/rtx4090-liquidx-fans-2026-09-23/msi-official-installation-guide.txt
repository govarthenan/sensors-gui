"SUPRIM LIQUID (Closed Loop) Installation

1. Turn off the power, and disconnect the power cable. Open the case of your computer.
2. Remove the expansion brackets required to place your graphics card.
3. Place the card directly over the PCI EXPRESS slot
4. Carefully insert the card into the PCI EXPRESS slot until the card is fully seated. You
   will likely hear a click when it is locked in place. Note: Inserting the graphics card
   into a wrong slot or using incorrect orientation may damage the card. Refer to your
   motherboard manual for further information if necessary.
5. Secure the card with a bracket screw.
6. Align the graphics card's radiator to the available ventilation holes of the computer
   case. (the radiator placement may vary per computer case)
7. Secure the radiator to the computer case with the supplied screws. Note: Rotate the tubes
   to a proper position for the following installation in the case.
8. Connect the required 16-pin power connector to the graphics card.
9. Close the case, re-connect the power cable and turn on the PC."

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:
1. The complete 9-step official installation procedure never instructs the installer to
   plug in, unplug, or route any fan cable, or to connect anything to a motherboard fan
   header. The only cable step in the whole guide is step 8, the 16-pin GPU power connector.
   This is consistent with the radiator-to-card fan cable being made and connected at the
   factory, not something a normal installation touches — which is why the MSI forum owner
   (msi-forum-radiator-fan-problem.txt) had to physically unplug an already-made connection
   at the radiator, rather than "plug in" a fan cable as part of setup.
2. This document is a generic multi-product leaflet (it is not captioned or numbered for the
   Liquid X specifically, and a photo caption inside even reads "MICROSOFT RTX 3060 Ti" —
   almost certainly a template artifact from a different product's version of this same
   leaflet). It is kept as the best "official manual" found, but is not Liquid-X-exclusive
   and gives no fan-specific information at all, unlike the reviews saved alongside it.
