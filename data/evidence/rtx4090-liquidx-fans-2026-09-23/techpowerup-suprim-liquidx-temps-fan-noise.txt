WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:
1. TechPowerUp reports exactly ONE "RPM" number per test condition for this card (1197 RPM
   gaming / 1105 RPM Quiet-BIOS gaming), not a per-fan breakdown, and does not state which
   NVML/GPU-Z fan index that number comes from. This is consistent with Tom's Hardware's
   explicit statement that this is not knowable from GPU-Z output alone (see
   tomshardware-suprim-liquidx-review-page6.txt).
2. This machine's own exp-0013 measurement under load gave fan0 = 1205 RPM and fan1 = 1520
   RPM. TechPowerUp's single reported figure, 1197 RPM (default Gaming BIOS), is close to
   this machine's fan0 (1205 RPM) and noticeably below fan1 (1520 RPM). This is numerically
   suggestive that GPU-Z's default/first-reported fan reading tracks the SLOWER of the two
   channels on this machine, i.e. fan0 — but it is an inference from proximity of numbers
   across two different physical cards under different load conditions, not a stated mapping,
   and is not treated as established fact.
3. The idle-row footnote ("the pump is running at minimal RPMs when idle, but not fully
   stopped") describes the PUMP, not the fans, and does not bear on the two-fan question.
