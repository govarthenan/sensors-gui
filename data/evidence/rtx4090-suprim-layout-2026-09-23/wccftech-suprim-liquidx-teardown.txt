"Unlike the massive monstrosities that the air coolers are in, the RTX 4090 SUPRIM Liquid X
comes in a very attractive 2-slot design. The card is a semi-hybrid design with a pump that
leads to a 240mm AIO radiator and a singular Torx 5.0 fan with axial tech that further helps
to dissipate heat. The RTX 4090 SUPRIM Liquid X measures 280x140x43mm while the radiator
(with fans installed) measures 274x121x55mm. The whole thing weighs in at just 2353g."

"Coming to the fans, the card actually features the latest fan designs based on the Torx 5.0
system. The singular fan features a ring-based design to allow for higher airflow to be
channeled within the main heatsink. [...] The fan body has three blades that form three sets
and each fan has three sets of them that make up a total of 9 fan blades."

"With the outside of the card done, I will now start taking a glance at what's beneath the
hood of the SUPRIM Liquid X. Unlike the SUPRIM X, the Liquid X is a dual-slot design and as
such, it doesn't use a huge aluminum fin block. It is also a liquid-cooled variant and as
such, it has a fully enclosed body with just two tubes leading into the central chamber."

"The pump makes use of a micro-fin copper base which has a large central GPU contact surface
and four exterior arms with a thicker surface to make direct contact with the 12 memory
modules that are featured on the RTX 4090. Each arm has thermal pads featured on it to make
sure that the heat is dissipated effectively."

"MSI adds extra protection to its impressive PCB by including a rugged anti-bending plate.
This also acts as additional memory and MOSFET cooling plate while the PWM heatsink with
micro fins keeps the VRM cool under stressful conditions. The 240mm radiator comes with MSI's
latest MEG Silent GALE P12 fans [...]"

"I/O on the graphics card sticks with the reference scheme which includes three Display Port
1.4a & a single HDMI 2.1 port."

"There's also a dual-BIOS switch on the card which comes pre-configured with Silent & Gaming
modes. [...] The limits are 450W for the silent and 480W (with 530W Power Limit) for the
gaming profile."

"MSI makes use of a 26+2 phase PWM design that is made up of high-quality components such as
HCI or High-Efficiency Carbony Inductors, SPS (Smart Power Stages), and hardened defense
fuse." [heading immediately above: "MSI GeForce RTX 4090 SUPRIM Liquid X Teardown:"]

CAUTION — internal inconsistency in this source, kept rather than silently corrected:
1. This page's backplate paragraph says almost word for word what page 8 (the air SUPRIM X
   section) says: "The graphics card also comes with a compact PCB design which means that
   the shroud, heatsink, and backplate are all extended beyond the PCB. The third fan blows
   air through the heatsink and blows it out from the cutouts that are situated at the very
   end of the backplate." This sentence mentions a "third fan", but the Liquid X (per this
   same page) has only one fan on its cold-plate/pump assembly plus separate radiator fans —
   it does not have three shroud fans. This looks like a copy/paste leftover from the air
   SUPRIM X section of the same article, so it is NOT treated as evidence about the Liquid
   X's own PCB/cooler length in the README.
2. This page states "530 Power Limit" for the Liquid X's gaming-mode maximum, while the air
   SUPRIM X section of the very same article (wccftech-suprimx-air-teardown.txt) states
   "550 power limit" / "up to 550 Watts" for the same 480 W gaming-mode default. This
   machine's own probe (data/probes/2026-09-23-nvidia-smi-q.txt) shows a 150-530 W range,
   matching the number given here for the Liquid X, not the number given for the air SUPRIM X
   elsewhere in this same article.
