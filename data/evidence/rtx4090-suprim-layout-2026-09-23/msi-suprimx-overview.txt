"The second iteration of SUPRIM inherits the exterior design philosophy of its predecessor,
with a brushed metal cover, octagon-shaped fan cutouts and chevrons around each fan."

"TRI-FROZR 3S THERMAL DESIGN" / "Upgraded fans, airflow control, and other thermal innovation
make TRI-FROZR 3S the best air-cooling solution yet from MSI."

"TORX FAN 5.0 incorporates design improvements that result in +23% increased airflow compared
to an axial fan."

"The resulting increased airflow is +10% compared to TORX FAN 4.0 and +23% compared to an
axial fan."

"Ring arcs link sets of three fan blades that focus airflow into the heatsink. Blades tilt 22
degrees to maintain high-pressure airflow even at slower rotational speeds."

"The trailing end of each ring arc folds inward to reduce turbulence and drag."

"A fan cowl extends a bit beyond the general enclosure to lengthen the wind's passageway,
allowing airflow to stabilize and move smoother through the fans."

"ZERO FROZR — The fans completely stop when temperatures are relatively low, eliminating all
noise when active cooling is not needed. The fans will automatically start spinning again
[when temperatures rise]."

"Two sets of durable ball bearings spin the TORX FANs for years of intense and [reliable
use]." [This describes the bearing design inside each fan, not how the fans are wired for
PWM/tach control — see README for why this must not be read as "2 fan control groups".]

"TRI FROZR 3S Thermal Design — TORX Fan 5.0: Fan blades linked by ring arcs and a fan cowl
